# Algorithm 6 — Prevention, support, and repair

## Aim

Help accountable people consider responses to a credible concern about harm, prioritizing safety, assistance, prevention, and repair. This develops `Sources/Algo-Justice` while rejecting automated risk-based punishment or restriction.

## Non-authority rule

This procedure does not determine guilt, predict an individual's dangerousness, open investigations, assign penalties, restrict liberty, or replace emergency services, courts, safeguarding bodies, or professional judgment. Any immediate safety response belongs to the legally authorized human service.

## Procedure

1. **Clarify what is known:** Separate verified events, allegations, interpretations, and predictions. Preserve relevant evidence lawfully. Do not infer guilt from group statistics, identity, poverty, or an opaque risk score.
2. **Attend to people affected:** Identify immediate needs and safety preferences; offer accessible support. Do not force mediation or restorative processes on affected people.
3. **Examine context and capacity:** Consider environmental drivers, unmet needs, accessibility, and whether voluntary support could reduce risk. Do not treat deprivation as culpability.
4. **Generate proportionate options:** Consider prevention, information, voluntary assistance, safeguarding, and repair. For restrictions, identify the lawful basis, necessity, evidence, proportionality, least restrictive effective alternative, time limit, and independent human review.
5. **Protect rights:** Ensure representation and due process where required, privacy, non-discrimination, notice, reasons, appeal, and remedy. Do not treat predicted recurrence as proof of intent or a sufficient reason for coercion.
6. **Plan repair and prevention:** Where appropriate and freely chosen, identify support for affected people, restitution or restoration options, and systemic changes to reduce recurrence.
7. **Review and learn:** Monitor whether the agreed human-authorized action addresses the concern and creates new harm. End restrictions promptly when their legal and evidentiary basis ends; record lessons and dissent.

## Output

A neutral case-support note: known/contested facts, immediate needs, context, voluntary options, rights and procedural safeguards, accountable human authority, uncertainties, and review/appeal route. No individual risk score or disposition recommendation.
