# Algorithm 3 — Needs and resource support

## Aim

Help identify possible unmet basic needs and organize voluntary, dignified support options. This extends `Sources/Algo-Resources` while avoiding unvalidated automated eligibility rules, intrusive surveillance, or compelled redistribution.

## Inputs

A request for support or a lawfully obtained service-level needs assessment; the person's stated needs and preferences; accessibility and local context; service availability; uncertainty and data permissions. Do not infer need from social media, proxies, or unrelated data.

## Procedure

1. **Ask, do not presume:** Invite the person to state needs, priorities, accessibility requirements, and preferred contact/support. Explain data use and offer a non-digital route.
2. **Check immediate safety:** If the person reports urgent danger or a time-critical health/safety need, refer promptly to the appropriate accountable human service under applicable procedures. Do not let the model adjudicate an emergency.
3. **Map needs by category:** With consent and appropriate safeguards, consider food, shelter, healthcare, water/sanitation, energy, safety, communication, mobility, and participation. Treat categories as prompts, not universal thresholds.
4. **Identify options:** Present available voluntary services, cash or in-kind support, accessibility adjustments, and community resources. Explain eligibility rules set by legitimate human institutions; do not invent thresholds.
5. **Respect choice:** Ask which options are acceptable. Declining a suggested service is not evidence of deception, low contribution, or lack of deservingness.
6. **Handle scarcity transparently:** If resources are limited, refer to the public allocation process and its human decision-maker. Surface urgency, uncertainty, relevant criteria, and appeal. The model cannot seize, redirect, or withhold resources.
7. **Confirm and follow up:** With permission, check whether support was received and whether it met the person's stated need. Correct inaccurate records; retain only necessary data for a defined period.

## Invariants

- No labor or contribution condition for essentials.
- No cross-resource arithmetic that treats food, healthcare, housing, autonomy, or safety as interchangeable.
- No automated hoarding accusation, forced redistribution, or denial of assistance.
- In a genuinely scarce service, follow lawful, public, nondiscriminatory rules with human review and appeal.

## Output

A person-controlled support summary: stated needs, requested options, service contacts, unresolved gaps, consent and data choices, appeal route, and optional follow-up. Never produce a deservingness score.
