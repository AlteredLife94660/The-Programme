# Algorithm 5 — Power concentration review

## Aim

Surface possible concentrations of wealth, influence, information, infrastructure, or institutional/algorithmic authority for human review. This develops `Sources/Algo-Decay` without adopting an automatic universal decay rate or authorizing compulsory seizure.

## Procedure

1. **Define the unit and decision:** State which organization, system, or institution is being assessed, the alleged power channel, the legitimate public purpose, and who will review the findings.
2. **Map control and dependencies:** Using lawful, proportionate evidence, identify control over essential resources, data, infrastructure, communication reach, agenda-setting, and decision authority. Include indirect and shared control where evidence supports it.
3. **Assess practical effects:** Ask whether the concentration materially limits others' choices, access, competition, privacy, dissent, or ability to challenge decisions. Distinguish scale from abuse; large size alone is not proof of harm.
4. **Check accountability:** Examine transparency, separation of duties, conflicts of interest, independent oversight, security, appeal, and exit/replaceability. A second AI is not automatically independent.
5. **Compare safeguards:** Offer lawful, proportionate, least-restrictive options for accountable human institutions to consider—such as disclosure, interoperability, procurement safeguards, separation of duties, competition policy, term/scope limits, or strengthened oversight.
6. **Test side effects:** Evaluate possible losses to privacy, security, essential service continuity, legitimate collective projects, smaller groups, and civil liberties. Assess who bears costs.
7. **Recommend review, not enforcement:** Provide evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, and an independent review path. Do not automatically reduce wealth, influence, reach, or authority.

## Invariants

- No universal exponential wealth/influence/authority decay imposed by the algorithm.
- No surveillance or personal-data collection without lawful purpose and necessity.
- No censorship or restriction based solely on a label of destabilizing speech. Target demonstrable harmful conduct only through legitimate rules and due process.
- No agent may unilaterally change its own authority or another actor's rights.

## Output

Power map; evidence and limitations; affected parties; potential benefits/harms of safeguards; lawful human options; appeal/oversight; review date. Label unverified concerns as allegations or hypotheses, not facts.
