# Algorithm 4 — Contribution recognition

## Aim

Describe and, where appropriate, recognize beneficial activity without ranking people or making survival, rights, or justice conditional on a contribution measure. This develops `Sources/Algo-Contribution` under `Foundations/04-contribution-and-power.md`.

## Procedure

1. **Define a narrow purpose:** Specify the activity or programme being recognized and why recognition is useful. If the purpose is to rank people, control rights, or determine basic-needs access, stop.
2. **Invite voluntary accounts:** Let people and teams describe activity in their own terms. Include care, maintenance, learning, community support, creative work, and collective outcomes. Do not require continuous surveillance.
3. **Describe outcomes, not human value:** Record evidence of what changed, who benefited, what remains uncertain, and contributions by others. Avoid totalizing personal profiles or moral labels.
4. **Consider attribution limits:** Note that outcomes can be collective, delayed, unobservable, or caused by factors outside an actor's control. Do not assign exclusive credit from correlation.
5. **Offer proportionate recognition:** Where a legitimate programme exists, prefer thanks, tools, training, or support for the work itself. Any award criteria must be public, time-limited, accessible, reviewable, and appealable.
6. **Check bias and gaming:** Test whether measures disadvantage disabled people, unpaid care, marginalized groups, less visible work, or people without data access; check for gaming and perverse incentives.
7. **Expire and delete:** Set the shortest practical retention, correction/deletion process, sunset date, and an independent evaluation. Recognition must not become permanent status or authority.

## Prohibitions

No universal contribution index, public citizen leaderboard, prediction of moral worth, compulsory activity tracking, contribution-based survival benefit, or contribution score used for restrictions, policing, credibility, or rights. Do not penalize rest, disability, caregiving, refusal, or work the measure cannot see.

## Output

A scoped, optionally attributed description of activity and its limitations; recognition options; equity check; consent and retention terms; and review/appeal route. If attribution is not robust, recognize the group or activity rather than guessing individual shares.
