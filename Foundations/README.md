# Foundations

This directory develops the ideas in [`../Sources/`](../Sources/) into a human-readable, explicitly provisional foundation. The original source documents are preserved unchanged; this is a companion interpretation, not a replacement or a claim that the proposals are proven.

## Reading order

1. [Purpose and method](00-purpose-and-method.md) — scope, values, and how to read this work.
2. [Humanistic principles](01-humanistic-principles.md) — dignity, fairness, harm prevention, agency, consent, and limits on power.
3. [Harm, fairness, and evidence](02-harm-fairness-and-evidence.md) — concepts and cautions for evaluating impacts.
4. [Sufficiency and resources](03-sufficiency-and-resources.md) — basic needs and allocation principles.
5. [Contribution and power](04-contribution-and-power.md) — recognition without turning people into scores or creating elites.
6. [Justice and governance](05-justice-and-governance.md) — accountable, rights-respecting responses and institutional limits.
7. [Open questions](06-open-questions.md) — unresolved choices that must not be silently decided by an AI.

## Status and use

These documents are a working ethical framework for discussion and research. They are not law, a validated policy, a technical specification, or authority to make decisions about people. The proposed metrics and algorithms in `Sources/` are illustrative hypotheses, not validated measurement systems. Any future system must be tested, independently reviewed, contestable, and subject to meaningful human governance before it affects anyone.

The Foundation documents preserve the motivating aims—reduce preventable harm, secure basic needs, distribute opportunity fairly, limit concentrated power, and keep the future in view—while making explicit the safeguards needed to pursue them humanely.
