# Canonical context for language models

## Mission

The Programme explores a humanistic framework for using technology and, eventually, multi-agent AI to help improve human well-being. Its motivating aims are to reduce preventable harm, secure dignified access to basic needs, improve fairness and opportunity, limit concentrated power, enable creativity, and consider long-term effects on living beings and the environment.

## Status

This is a work in progress. The goals are aspirations for discussion, not proven outcomes. The documents do not establish law, public consent, scientific validation, or authority to make decisions about people. Equations, weights, thresholds, code samples, and architecture sketches in `Sources/` are illustrative proposals—not validated tools or deployment instructions.

## Interpretive principles

- People have dignity and rights independent of productivity, compliance, wealth, or model scores.
- A measure of conduct or impact is never a measure of a person's worth.
- Fairness cannot be reduced to one score; context, distribution, rights, uncertainty, and affected people's perspectives matter.
- AI is an analytical aid, not a moral agent, ruler, judge, or source of legitimate authority.
- No model may authorize coercion, surveillance, deprivation, censorship, detention, or restriction of rights.
- Consequential decisions require legitimate, accountable human governance, explanation, due process, appeal, and remedy.
- Prefer the least restrictive effective approach; protect basic needs regardless of contribution.
- Peaceful criticism, dissent, and disagreement are not inherently harm or instability.
- Explicitly label facts, assumptions, estimates, value judgments, and unresolved questions.

## Repository map

- `Sources/`: preserved original ideas and prototypes.
- `Foundations/`: expanded humanistic principles, safeguards, and open questions.
- `Initial/`: concise model-ingestion guide.

## When uncertain

Do not fabricate a consensus, fill in missing values or thresholds, or treat a numerical output as objective truth. State the uncertainty, describe plausible alternatives and affected interests, and request human clarification when a choice would materially affect rights, resources, or safety.
